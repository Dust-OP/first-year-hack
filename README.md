# Reference Loop

A static website introducing the reference-loop research workflow. Open `index.html` locally; no build step or dependencies are required.

The [reference-loop](reference-loop/) directory contains the skill, helper scripts, references and tests. Read its [README](reference-loop/README.md) and [SKILL.md](reference-loop/SKILL.md) before use. The source-tool notes include historical device-specific examples; check availability on your own device. Node.js 22 or later is required for the JavaScript helpers.

## Deployment

Deploy to Vercel only when explicitly requested or when needed to show a live result. Pushing to GitHub does not currently trigger a Vercel deployment.

## KCL First Year Hackason
## 🌟 Project Overview
For centuries, the compass has guided explorers through unfamiliar oceans and unknown territories. It helped people discover new places, overcome uncertainty, and find their way forward.

But in today’s interconnected world, we face a different challenge.

**We know how to find places. But how do we find the right people?**

Introducing **WAYRISE**, a hackathon project that reimagines the traditional compass as a symbol of human connection.

Instead of pointing toward geographical north, WAYRISE helps users navigate toward people who can support their personal, academic, and professional journeys.
Our mission is simple:

**Find your direction. Connect with the right people. Rise together.**

---
**🎯 The Problem**

Finding the right person for guidance or collaboration can be surprisingly difficult.
Students, entrepreneurs, and aspiring professionals may struggle to identify relevant mentors, discover experienced experts, or connect with people who share their goals.

Traditional networking can be overwhelming, fragmented, and time-consuming.

We believe finding the right connection should feel as intuitive as following a compass.

---
**💡 Our Solution**

WAYRISE introduces a compass-inspired approach to discovering meaningful professional connections.
The project aims to help users:

- Discover people with relevant knowledge and experience.
- Find potential mentors and industry experts.
 -Explore opportunities for collaboration.
- Navigate their journey toward personal and professional growth.
  
Rather than focusing only on destinations, WAYRISE focuses on the people who help us reach them.

---
**🎨 Design Concept**

**Ancient Wisdom Meets Modern Technology**

WAYRISE is inspired by the evolution of navigation.

Our design combines the timeless symbolism of the traditional compass with a modern digital experience.

**The Compass:** Represents direction, discovery, and guidance.
**Gold:** Symbolises ambition, opportunity, and achievement.
**Midnight Blue:** Represents trust, knowledge, and exploration.

**Human Connections:** Transform the concept of navigation from finding geographical destinations into discovering meaningful relationships.

The result is a design philosophy rooted in the past but focused on the future.

---
**🚀 Our Vision**

We envision a world where no one has to navigate their ambitions alone.

Whether someone is beginning their career, developing an innovative idea, or exploring an unfamiliar field, the right connection can make all the difference.

WAYRISE is built around one belief:
**Success isn’t only about knowing where to go. It’s about finding the people who help you get there.**
