Compass Design Atlas

Every compass front end found in the research, with a picture of how it looks. The screenshots were taken on 21 Nov 2026 in a headless browser with no compass sensor, so live apps often show their “enable sensor” or no-signal state. Where a project has its own screenshot in its README, that view is offered too. Click any image to enlarge it.

About this atlas
  • Licence tags: “copyable” means MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-style, CC0 or CC BY (copy with attribution). “Reference only” means GPL, AGPL, no licence or commercial: borrow the idea, not the code or art. “Not code” marks apps, articles and products.
  • Status: “source read” means the implementing code was opened at a stated commit; “thread read” means the post and replies were read; “lead only” means it was found but not inspected.
  • Gaps: Reddit and CodePen could not be read automatically. Some sites refused screenshots; those cards link out instead. Nothing here was tested on a phone.
  • Screenshots are of third-party sites and are shown for design reference only. Full evidence: new-project/findings/compass-frontend-design/ in the team repo.
