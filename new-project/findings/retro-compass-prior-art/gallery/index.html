59 compasses that already exist

Every project we found that reinvents the compass, with what each one looks like. Pointing at places, people or north has all been done. Nobody has made a compass that understands a free-text request, checks live facts first, and changes target when those facts change.

Closest to our idea

#4 X Marks the Spot (AI picks a hidden place, no prize) and #49 Terra (intention to trail; builders say it doesn't work).

Why the gap is real

#55 pointed to a liquor store that had closed 4 hours earlier. #56's "what you want most" ended up hard-coded to an airport.

Demo risk

#7 CampMap's phone compass was off by more than 100° indoors. Plan a simulated-heading demo mode.
