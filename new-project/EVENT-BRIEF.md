# Event brief and team decisions

FirstYearHack 2026 (KCL Tech), 21 Nov 2026, Bush House. Submissions and judging start at 18:00 ([event page](https://luma.com/firstyearhack)).
This file records the brief as the team received it and the decisions made so far. Research lives in [`findings/`](findings/).

## Tracks (as given on the day)

**Track 1: Creative Tech — Code Meets Culture.** Combine technology with music, art, film, fashion or storytelling.
- Judges look for creative, unusual ideas, connections to culture, and cool, engaging experiences.
- Examples: Spotify listening history as artwork, an interactive adventure game about university life, an AI outfit generator from wardrobe photos.

**Track 2: Retro Remix — Old Tech, New Tricks.** Take something old, such as a classic game, gadget, website or trend, and reinvent it for 2026.
- Judges look for reinvention of old technology, and surprising but faithful modern adaptations.
- Examples: multiplayer Snake, a 2000s-style social network for university students, a modern Tamagotchi living in a browser tab.

**Bonus prize: Best Use of Snowflake.** This is a sponsor prize, not a third main track. Use Snowflake technologies such as its datasets, the Cortex Code CLI or other platform features.
