Privacy notice

Career Compass is a student prototype built at KCL FirstYearHack on 21 November 2026. This page describes what the app's code does today. It has not had a legal review.

What other people see

  • On the compass: only signed-in people whose app reports a position within 500 m of yours, and only while you are visible.
  • In the People tab: anyone you have invited, or who has invited you, keeps your name, what you do, your LinkedIn link and your tags in their list. That does not depend on distance, and it stays after you go invisible, sign out or decline.
  • Your name, what you do, your LinkedIn link, and what you offer and are looking for.
  • A direction and a distance to you, measured from the position their app reports. The app never sends your coordinates to another user and shows no map, but a direction and a distance from a known spot are enough to work out where you are to within GPS accuracy, and the app does not check that the other person is really where their app says.
  • If you sign in with email (through Logto or with a code we email you), your email address is never shown to other users. The name box is pre-filled with the part of your address before the @; change it if you do not want others to see that.

There are no adverts, analytics or tracking cookies.

If you reply to a sign-in email, the reply goes to hello@wayrise.app, which forwards it (through Cloudflare's email routing) to a team member's inbox.

Why we use it

Only to point your compass at people nearby and let you invite them. We ask for your agreement before you start, and you can change your mind at any time. Your phone asks you separately before it shares your location; an iPhone also asks before sharing its motion sensors.

Demo people

During demos the app can place made-up “demo people” around you. They are not real users and are marked as demo data in the database.

Your choices

  • Go invisible, or sign out, at any time: both buttons are under “Demo tools and what runs underneath” on the compass screen. Refusing location access does not hide you: the app then uses a fixed demo position (Bush House) and still shows your profile to people near it.
