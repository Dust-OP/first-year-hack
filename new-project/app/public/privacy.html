Privacy notice

Career Compass is a student prototype built at KCL FirstYearHack on 21 November 2026. This page describes what the app's code does today. It has not had a legal review.

What other people see

  • Only people within 500 m who also have the app open, and only while you are visible.
  • Your name, what you do, your LinkedIn link, and what you offer and are looking for.
  • A direction and a distance to you. Your coordinates never leave the server, so nobody sees you on a map.
  • If you sign in with email, your email address is never shown to other users.

There are no adverts, analytics or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

Only to point your compass at people nearby and let you invite them. We ask for your agreement before you start, and you can change your mind at any time. Your phone also asks you separately before it shares your location or motion sensors.

Demo people

During demos the app can place made-up “demo people” around you. They are not real users and are marked as demo data in the database.
