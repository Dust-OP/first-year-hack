THE METHOD

From curiosity
to clarity.

Start with a real decision. Follow the evidence that matters, then leave a trail someone else can use.

01 / FRAME

Scope the question

Define the decision, the target context, and what evidence would settle it. Check existing research before starting again.

02 / DISCOVER

Follow the sources

Read original documentation, implementation and relevant discussions. Look beyond the first result, including specialist communities when they help.

03 / VERIFY

Test what matters

Exercise behavior when the decision depends on it. Distinguish what was observed, what was read, and what remains uncertain.

04 / SHARE

Make it actionable

Record the recommendation, evidence, limitations and next check. Keep the useful trail so the work can be revisited.

EVIDENCE, NOT ASSUMPTIONS

Know what
you know.

A source is a starting point. A reliable conclusion makes its basis and its limits visible.

01Verified live

Exercised on the actual target.

02Verified locally

Tested in a local environment, with its limits noted.

03Read in source or specification

Supported by code or documentation, without claiming execution.

04Inferred or unverified

A reasoned conclusion or an open gap, labelled clearly.
